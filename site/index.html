How it works and what to know

Real mode: orders go from your browser to Hyperliquid, signed by a trading-only key you approve; it cannot withdraw. Your funds stay in your own Hyperliquid account and this site never holds them. A 0.03% site fee applies to real orders. Real mode covers SOL, BTC and ETH, is in beta, and is not available to US persons. Trading with leverage can lose your whole margin.

Paper mode: no real funds are involved, and nothing here is audited. Prices, one-minute candles and funding rates come from Hyperliquid's public feed: its main perps for crypto and its XYZ market for stock perps. Your paper positions are marked to those prices; paper funding is not charged. If the feed cannot be reached, prices are a random simulation; the chip at the top says which.

Each trader faces a house account that takes the other side. When the engine certifies an account below maintenance margin, the house closes it at the oracle price; if that is refused, the engine's own liquidation runs. Liquidation prices shown are estimates. The engine is Percolator at commit 37cb14e, Apache-2.0, compiled to WebAssembly without changes.
